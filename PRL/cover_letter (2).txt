Cover Letter — Pattern Recognition Letters

Dear Editors,

We submit our manuscript, "Post-processing and evaluation artifacts dominate
measured losses in table structure recognition: a cross-paradigm audit," for
consideration in Pattern Recognition Letters.

Table structure recognition (TSR) papers overwhelmingly report improvements in
model components, yet the measured numbers conflate three sources: the model,
the post-processing pipeline, and the evaluation machinery itself. This letter
reports, to our knowledge, the first cross-paradigm audit that decomposes those
sources. Across four public systems (SPLERGE, TableFormer/docling v2.1.0,
TableTransformer, LORE-TSR), we replace each heuristic post-processor with an
exact combinatorial formulation under identical inputs and objectives, and we
quantify four artifacts: destructive merge heuristics (27-34% cell
destruction, fully repaired by exact set packing), objective-vs-solver
dissociation (unnormalized greedy collapse to 27.5% of exact recall;
normalized greedy matches the ILP), a threshold-policy recall deficit (LORE:
31.2% to 90.5% recall from one threshold change at 99.2% precision), and
evaluation circularity (TableFormer's ground-truth-derived PDF text layer;
+406 TP inflation from snap-to-ground-truth matching over snap-disabled
matching). We further show
that TEDS-S rewards over-pruning systematically, not anecdotally: the
destructive heuristic outscores exact selection on 57 of 100 tables (Wilcoxon
p=2.5e-3; a mean +0.059 TEDS-S advantage purchased with 500 destroyed cells),
7% of tables lose over half their cells yet still score 0.80 or higher, and
case studies include a table where a heuristic retaining 3.9% of cells scores
near-perfect TEDS-S (0.9265).

Why PRL: the contribution is a concise, measurement-focused finding about how
pattern recognition systems should be evaluated, with direct methodological
consequences for a large and active subfield. All N=100 analyses are labelled
exploratory; a 1,000-table confirmatory replication is pre-specified
(pre-committed hypotheses, TOST margins, power analysis, pinned seeds and
dependencies) and will be executed before acceptance.

This manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not
under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the submission and
declare no competing interests. All traces, per-table results, a frozen
results ledger, the pre-specified replication protocol, and code are
available at
https://github.com/maxprogrammer007/major_project_csvtu.
